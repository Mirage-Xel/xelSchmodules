On the Subject of X, Y, and Zee

A kitten turning into a cow wouldn't even rank in the top ten strangest things you've encountered as part of this career... this week.

Pressing the cube will cause it to move independently on each of the three axes. Translate each axis's movements into two sets of two letters each as follows, representing each letter as a nonzero three-trit ternary number equal to its position in the alphabet. Pressing the cube again will generate a new set of letters.

  • No movement represents 0, movement in the positive direction represents 1, and movement in the negative direction represents 2. The status light is in the positive quadrant of the XZ plane, and movement away from the bomb is positive.
  • 1 and 2 are assigned durations that are twice and three times the duration that 0 is assigned, respectively, bearing in mind that speeds are randomized per axis. After transmitting one of the letters, the cube will cancel out its net motion on that axis before transmitting the second.

The set of letters obtained from the directions and the one obtained from the durations can each be anagrammed into one of the words from the list below with an extra letter. Press the button labeled with the axis that did not contain an extra letter to solve the module. Pressing an incorrect button will cause a strike and disable the buttons until the cube is pressed again.
